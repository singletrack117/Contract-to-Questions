A&GEL Checklist-to-Questions

Flatten a checklist into a leveled list of questions. Drop in a disclosure schedule, requirements checklist, or any numbered document and this mini-app turns every requirement into one question — each tagged with its level (top → 5th) so you can group, fold, or compare line-by-line against your precedents.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Word checklists laid out as tables (e.g. a “Disclosure Requirement” schedule) are read column-aware — only the requirement text is pulled, and its PART / 1. / (a) / (i) / (A) numbering is reconstructed into levels. Word and PDF parsing use on-demand libraries (JSZip, mammoth.js, pdf.js); if those cannot load, paste the text instead.
